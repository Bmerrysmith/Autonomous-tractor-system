## Summary

Debugged why WeedDet V7 (custom RetinaNet-style rice detector, riceseg-pretrained backbone, leakage-safe COCO split) trained to AP@50 ≈ 0 despite decreasing loss. Through a one-variable-at-a-time test campaign (TEST_LOG.md), four compounding root causes were found and fixed: an ATSS classification ignore-band leak, over-aggressive gradient clipping, anchors mis-scaled to plant sizes, and catastrophic forgetting of the pretrained backbone. Current best: **stable val AP@50 = 0.1040** (frozen riceseg backbone, T7) vs scratch baseline 0.0450; the remaining ceiling is structural — a ~300-det/img false-positive flood traced to the ATSS positive-assignment geometry — with fixes designed but not yet implemented (T10/T11).

### Architecture/Design Notes
- Model: WeedDet (Peng et al. 2022 reimplementation) — Det-ResNet-50 (strides 4/8/16, verified), eFPN P3–P5, ERetinaHead (64-ch, LSC k=7), 12 anchors/location, ATSS assignment, VFL(+hard targets)+SmoothL1+CIoU
- Settled config after campaign: `atss_all_neg=True`, `cls_hard_target=True`, `GRAD_CLIP=10.0`, `ANCHOR_BASE_SCALE=6`, `BASE_LR=5e-4`, 20–32-ep cosine, `FREEZE_BACKBONE=True` (riceseg runs), EMA 0.999, batch 2, seed 42
- Eval protocol (fixed): pycocotools, score_thr 0.01, hard NMS 0.5, maxDets 300, selection on val AP@50; test split reserved for exactly one final eval

### Known Issues
- **FP flood (primary ceiling):** det/img pinned at ~300 at score_thr 0.01 in every run; AP@75 stuck ≤0.012. Diagnosed cause: ATSS positive set is ~9 anchors at the single GT-center cell (co-located-anchor distance ties; top-9-of-12 also systematically excludes ar=1.0); adjacent same-texture anchors are hard negatives → head smears scores over all plant texture. Fixes designed, not implemented: T10 (per-location/cell top-k candidates), T11 (soft quality targets — newly viable since base-6 anchors give assignment IoUs 0.4–0.9)
