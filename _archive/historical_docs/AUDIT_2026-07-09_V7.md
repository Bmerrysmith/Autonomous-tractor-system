# In-Depth Audit — V7 notebook + weeddet_v6b.py + riceseg_pretrain.py (2026-07-09)

## Verdict in one line

Transfer learning is implemented correctly; the collapse is a **training-supervision bug in
`WeedDetLoss`**: the classification ignore band (`neg_iou_threshold=0.4`) leaks unsupervised anchors
which saturate to score 1.00 under the new hard targets, flooding eval with unregressed-anchor
false positives. EMA is exonerated (raw AP 0.0003 vs EMA 0.0010 — both dead).

## 2. Structural bug #1 — classification ignore band (PRIME SUSPECT)

`weeddet_v6b.py`:
- L770: `neg = ~pos & (max_iou < self.neg_iou_threshold)`  → non-positive anchors with IoU ≥ 0.4 are neither pos nor neg
- L801/L836: `ign = ~pos & ~neg`; `valid = ~ign` → those anchors get **zero classification gradient, ever**

Standard ATSS has **no ignore band** (all non-positives are negatives). With ~29 GT/image and 12
anchors/location at stride 4, hundreds of anchors per image sit in this band. Consequences:

- Run #1 (soft IoU targets ~0.06): positives barely pushed up → scores capped ~0.06–0.21, AP 0.0088.
- Run #2 (hard 1.0 targets): positives driven to 1.0; ignore-band anchors are featurally identical to
  positives, drift to 1.0 with **no negative pressure** → at eval (no band exists at inference) they
  fire at 1.00. Regression is trained only on positives, so these anchors emit **raw anchor shapes**.

**Visual confirmation (Cell 10):** FP boxes are ~5×27 px vertical slivers = the unregressed
ar=0.2 / stride-4 anchor (base 12 px, w=12·√0.2≈5.4, h=12/√0.2≈26.8), all at score exactly 1.00,
clustered on plant-like texture (distant canopy, water reflections), foreground plants missed.
Saturated-classifier + untouched-regression signature, exactly what the leak predicts.

**Fix (one line):** `neg = ~pos` in the ATSS branch. Then re-run overfit-16 → 20-epoch full gate.

## 3. Structural bug #2 — ATSS candidate selection degenerates under anchor ties

L731: `topk_idxs = distances[start:end].topk(k, dim=0, largest=False)` treats each of the 12
co-located anchors as a separate candidate. All 12 share one center → identical distances → topk(9)
returns 9 arbitrary anchors of the **single nearest cell** per level, instead of the 9 nearest
*locations* (ATSS paper semantics). Effects: positive pool collapses to ~1 cell/level per GT;
`mean+std` threshold (L744) is computed over near-identical IoUs (degenerate statistics); 3 of 12
anchor shapes at that cell are dropped by tie-break.

**Fix:** compute distances per location (`view(H*W, A)` per level or dedupe centers), topk over
locations, then admit that location's anchors (or its best-IoU anchor) as candidates.
